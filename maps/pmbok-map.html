READING THE FLOW

Lines show how the work hands off, from authorizing the job to closing it. Hover over a card, or focus it with the keyboard, to light up what feeds it and what it feeds. On a phone the lines give way to the Feeds → buttons on each card: tap a number to jump to the next process.

The flow is MC&C's reading of how industrial work moves. It is not PMI's published inputs and outputs.

THE SEVEN PERFORMANCE DOMAINS

1. Governance2. Scope3. Schedule4. Finance5. Stakeholders6. Resources7. Risk

HOW TO READ IT

Grid: read across for the life of the job, from Initiating to Closing; read down for one domain. Flow chart: follow the hand-offs from authorizing the job to closing it. On a phone, tap a number under Feeds → to jump to the next process. Every card flips. The red "Where it breaks" box is the gap MC&C looks for, and its link opens that track on The Mayormont Method poster.

WHAT CHANGED IN THE 8TH EDITION

The 8th Edition brings processes back after the 7th Edition dropped them: 40 processes instead of the 6th Edition's 49. Ten knowledge areas became seven performance domains: Integration, Quality and Procurement now sit under Governance, Communications joins Stakeholders, and Cost becomes Finance. Process groups are now called focus areas.
