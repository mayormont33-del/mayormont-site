1The flow: the Mayormont Method, run at our speed

Laid out as the Mayormont Method: process groups across, tracks down, every box numbered. The difference is time. Initiating and planning, which usually take weeks, are compressed into one 48-hour drill-down. Six tracks run in parallel, and every column ends at a signed gate.

Click a track (Money, People…) or any box to drill into it on the poster. Click a phase header to mark where the engagement stands.

CONVENTIONAL ASSESSMENTWeeks of discovery before anyone sees a plan. Tracks studied one after another.
MC&C DRILL-DOWN48 hours to the Gap Brief. All six tracks drilled at once, each gap with a dollar figure and one owner.
THE GATESG0 at 48 hours, G1 at day 30, G2 to G4 at agreed milestones, G5 at close-out. No gate passes without signatures (forms F-01 to F-04).

2The loop: how we operate

The work never runs in a straight line. We find, then we fix, and every crossing passes the gate and the Four-Count before the next drill begins. The loop runs until there is no gap left to find.

Click any step to see where it sits on the poster.

3The fishbone: recovery of the enterprise

An Ishikawa diagram of why an enterprise bleeds. Each bone is one of our six tracks; the lines on it are the causes we find in the 48 hours. Each fix is the countermeasure the Command Phase puts in place.

Click a bone to drill into that track on the poster.
